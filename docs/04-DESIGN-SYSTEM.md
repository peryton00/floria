# Design System

## Brand
Botanical, premium, calm, editorial, natural, trustworthy and modern.

## Logo
Use the approved Floria botanical mark and wordmark. Variants: primary dark-green, black, white-on-dark, transparent SVG/PNG and compact mark. Do not redraw it.

## Typography
Editorial serif for major headings; clean sans-serif for body/UI. Use restrained font families and clear type roles.

## Color
Deep forest green primary, warm off-white/cream background, near-black text, muted natural neutrals, restrained warm accent. Use CSS design tokens. Avoid bright SaaS gradients.

## Layout
Mobile-first, generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, selective rounded cards, restrained shadows, product imagery dominant, accessible tap targets.

## Customer bottom nav
Home | Categories | Search | Orders | Account
Cart remains prominent in header/purchase flow.

## Test widths
320, 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1440px.

## States
Loading, empty, error, success, disabled, out-of-stock, permission-denied and appropriate poor-network feedback.

## Accessibility
Semantic HTML, keyboard support, visible focus, contrast, labels, alt text, mobile-friendly targets.
